Binance says fake job interviews used as part of $6.3bn crypto hack

Hackers stole a record $6.3 billion from Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, by using fake job interviews and a compromised internal AI tool to gain system access.

Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, revealed that hackers stole a record $6.3 billion (£4.7 billion) through a "sophisticated, multi-stage campaign" involving fake job interviews and a compromised internal AI tool. The initial findings indicate hackers used social engineering techniques, such as phishing and malicious software downloads disguised as job assignments, to compromise Binance engineers' computers. Perpetrators built trust with an engineer through a legitimate job-hunting platform, leading to bogus interviews and the download of malware. This malware, with "obfuscation techniques," evaded detection. In October, hackers leveraged the compromised engineer's computer and an internal AI tool to gain control over two other engineers' laptops, executing further attack steps. Binance called it a "serious and highly sophisticated security incident" and apologized for the disruption. The incident led to a global suspension of withdrawals, with customers withdrawing billions after services resumed. Binance has taken steps including working with law enforcement and setting aside over 100% of funds to cover the loss, with 98% of impacted token assets replenished. This is considered the largest crypto hack to date.

Rủi ro
The use of sophisticated social engineering and compromised internal tools highlights significant cybersecurity vulnerabilities within large organizations, posing a risk to user assets and trust.
Theo dõi
The ongoing efforts by Binance to work with law enforcement to trace and freeze affected assets, and the implementation of enhanced security measures, will be crucial to monitor.
Hàm ý
This record-breaking hack underscores the critical importance of robust cybersecurity, employee training against social engineering, and secure internal tool management for cryptocurrency exchanges and other tech companies handling sensitive assets.
