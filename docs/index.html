Claude chatbot maker set for $1.3tn stock market debut despite 'catastrophic' AI warning

Anthropic, maker of the Claude chatbot, is set for a stock market debut on Nasdaq with a potential valuation of up to $1.3 trillion, despite its own warnings about AI risks.

Anthropic, the AI company behind the Claude chatbot, is preparing for its stock market debut on New York's Nasdaq exchange, with a potential valuation of around $1.3 trillion (£1 trillion). The loss-making company aims to raise up to $64bn by offering shares between $70 and $78 each this month. This valuation, while substantial, is below the $2 trillion some backers predicted. The float comes amidst a volatile period for big tech, with the Nasdaq falling after US midterm elections due to concerns about AI development and data center buildouts. There are also broader fears about the billions invested in AI and its potential returns. Anthropic's revenues surged to $16.2bn in the first half of the year, up from $1.1bn. Fiona Cincotta, a senior market analyst, described the $64bn fundraising target as "fairly bullish" given current AI concerns. Anthropic, listing as ANT on November 19, will be the first fully AI-focused company to go public, serving as a test case for the technology's future. Its potential $1.3tn valuation is less than SpaceX's nearly $1.8tn valuation at its June listing.

The company's own warning about "catastrophic or existential risks to humanity" from AI, coupled with market volatility and concerns over AI investment returns, poses risks to its valuation and investor sentiment.
