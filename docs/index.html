Scandal-hit Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang quits, plunging AI chipmaking giant into turmoil

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has abruptly resigned amid allegations of extramarital affairs, causing the AI chipmaking giant to lose $550 billion in market value.

Jensen Huang, CEO of Nvidia, has resigned following revelations of alleged extramarital affairs, including one with a suspected Chinese spy, Alice Huang, and another with an Nvidia executive, Kimberly Powell. This scandal has led to Nvidia losing $550 billion (£415 billion) in stock market value in five days and prompted scrutiny from US lawmakers demanding answers from Huang regarding national security risks. Colette Kress has been appointed interim CEO, and the Department of Justice has not yet concluded whether Alice Huang was involved in espionage. Senators Elizabeth Warren and Chris Coons have publicly demanded Huang brief Congress, with a potential subpoena if he refuses.

Rủi ro
Nvidia faces national security concerns due to Huang's alleged affair with a suspected Chinese spy, potentially leading to further government intervention and investigations.
Theo dõi
The market will be watching for further developments regarding the US lawmakers' demands for Huang to brief Congress and the Department of Justice's conclusions on espionage allegations.
Hàm ý
The scandal highlights the significant impact of executive conduct on corporate stability and market valuation, especially for high-profile tech companies with geopolitical relevance.
