Faisal Islam: Why Jensen Huang proved too big to fire

Jensen Huang's reinstatement as Nvidia CEO, despite controversy, led to a $1 trillion increase in Nvidia's market value, highlighting his critical importance to the AI boom and global economy.

Jensen Huang was reinstated as Nvidia chief executive after admitting an affair, a decision influenced by President Trump's direct message to the board and a US government reassurance of no national security concerns. This comeback, which added one trillion dollars to Nvidia's stock market value, is seen as more significant than Sam Altman's or Steve Jobs' returns. Huang's influence is crucial for the US economy, the future of AI, and the geopolitical competition between the US and China, especially given his past advocacy for Chinese access to advanced Nvidia microchips. Nvidia's phenomenal sales, over $400 billion this year compared to $27 billion four years ago, and its role in financing other companies for data centers, underscore its importance to the AI boom, despite concerns about "circular financing" and public backlash against data centers.

Concerns about "circular financing" and public backlash against data centers and AI, as evident at the US midterm elections, could pose risks to the sustained growth of the AI industry.
