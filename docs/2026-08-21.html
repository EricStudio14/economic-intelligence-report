From the consortium's perspective, acquiring Steadfast offers a strategic entry or expansion into the Australian and New Zealand markets, leveraging Steadfast's established presence and broker network. This deal reflects the robust appetite for high-quality assets in the financial services sector, even amidst broader economic uncertainties, as investors seek stable, cash-generative businesses.
