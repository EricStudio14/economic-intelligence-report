Interest rates hiked to 4% in first increase in over three years

The Bank of England hiked interest rates to 4% from 3.75%, the first increase in over three years, to curb inflation driven by the US-Iran war's impact on oil prices.

The Bank of England has raised interest rates to 4% from 3.75%, marking the first increase in over three years, primarily to combat inflation fueled by the US-Iran war's effect on global oil prices. Governor Andrew Bailey stated this decision is not necessarily the start of a long series of hikes but warned that further increases are likely unless the Middle East conflict is resolved. The rate hike, supported by eight out of nine committee members, will increase borrowing costs for mortgages, loans, and credit cards, while also boosting returns on savings. Bailey emphasized that future decisions depend on progress in settling the Middle East conflict and the path of energy prices and inflation. Committee members Clare Lombardelli and Megan Greene also suggested further hikes might be necessary, while Alan Taylor viewed it as a "risk-management 'one-for-now' insurance hike."

Theo dõi
The resolution of the conflict in the Middle East and the trajectory of energy prices and inflation in the coming months will be key factors influencing future rate decisions.
Hàm ý
Businesses and consumers should anticipate higher borrowing costs and potentially higher returns on savings, while monitoring geopolitical developments for their impact on monetary policy.

WGC cho rằng đợt giảm này là nhịp điều chỉnh, không phải chu kỳ suy giảm kéo dài. Thị trường bạc cũng biến động mạnh, giá bạc quốc tế giảm 17% trong một tháng qua, còn trong nước giảm khoảng 25%.

Ngắn hạnGiá vàng có thể tiếp tục chịu áp lực bán và kiểm định vùng hỗ trợ 3.857 USD một ounce trong ngắn hạn.
