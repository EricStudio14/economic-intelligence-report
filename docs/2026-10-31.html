Crypto giant Binance attempting to recover $6.3bn stolen in hack linked to North Korea

Crypto giant Binance is attempting to recover $6.3 billion stolen in a hack linked to North Korea, raising concerns about customer fund coverage.

Binance, founded in China in 2017 with over 300 million registered users and more than $160 billion in assets under custody in 2024, is facing fears from users and the crypto industry about its ability to cover a $6.3 billion financial loss from a hack. The company has repeatedly stated that customer funds will be covered and that it is strong enough to weather the storm, but crypto history watchers recall similar assurances from FTX CEO Sam Bankman-Fried before its 2022 collapse. Experts warn against considering Binance's promise legally binding, suggesting litigation might be needed. TRM Labs and Chainalysis have high confidence in North Korean links to the attack, resembling previous incidents attributed to the Lazarus Group, which experts believe uses cybercrime to raise public funds for the impoverished nation.
