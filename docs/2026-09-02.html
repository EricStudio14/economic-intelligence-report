Historically, sustained increases in bond yields have often preceded periods of economic slowdown or recession, as higher interest rates dampen economic activity. This situation presents a dual challenge: central banks must balance controlling inflation with avoiding an overly restrictive monetary policy that could stifle growth, while investors navigate a landscape where both inflation and interest rate risks are prominent.
